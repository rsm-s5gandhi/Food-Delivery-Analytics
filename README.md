# Food Delivery Analytics: DoorDash vs Uber Eats 2020–2024

An interactive Tableau Public dashboard analyzing the US food delivery duopoly 
across 6 chart types, built entirely from real company filings and industry data.

## What's Inside
| Chart | Type | Story |
|---|---|---|
| Profitability Journey | Diverging bar | From $1.4B loss to first profit |
| US Market Share | Line chart | DoorDash 45% → 67%, Grubhub collapse |
| Unit Economics | Waterfall (Gantt) | Where every dollar goes |
| Category Mix | Diverging bar | DoorDash vs Uber Eats head to head |
| City Battleground | Filled state map | Who leads where |
| Courier Earnings | Slope chart | $4.2B → $18B in 4 years |

## Data Sources
- DoorDash SEC Form 8-K filings (Q1 2023 – Q1 2025)
- Uber Technologies SEC Form 8-K filings, Delivery segment
- Second Measure / Bloomberg industry market share reports
- DoorDash 2024 Annual Report
- Business of Apps aggregated statistics
